Our Services

Nine service lines covering the full building lifecycle — from soil investigation and structural design to vetting, BIM and site supervision.

A. Structural Engineering Services

Design of reinforced concrete buildings of all occupancy types, from residential houses to 20-floor high-rise structures.

  • Multi-storey commercial, mixed-use and high-rise structures (up to 20 floors)
  • Residential houses, plazas and apartment towers
  • Institutional buildings: schools, colleges, universities
  • Healthcare facilities: hospitals and clinics
  • Mosques, religious structures and community halls
  • Industrial and warehouse structures

B. Earthquake Engineering

Specialized seismic services for Pakistan's high seismic-risk zones.

  • Seismic vulnerability assessment and risk evaluation
  • Earthquake-resistant design per ASCE 7 / UBC / IBC
  • Performance-based seismic analysis
  • Non-linear dynamic (time-history) analysis
  • Seismic detailing and ductility design

C. Design Vetting (Third-Party Review)

Independent verification that gives owners, banks and authorities confidence in structural safety.

  • Independent verification of structural safety and adequacy
  • Design optimization for cost efficiency and structural integrity
  • Full compliance check with ACI, ASCE, IBC and PEC guidelines
  • Review of architectural-structural coordination and clash detection
  • Formal vetting reports for regulatory submission

D. Retrofitting & Rehabilitation

Strengthening and upgrading existing structures for safety and new use.

  • Strengthening of earthquake-damaged or deteriorating structures
  • Column jacketing, beam strengthening and shear wall addition
  • Assessment of cracks, settlement and concrete deterioration
  • Condition surveys and structural health reports
  • Structural upgrading for change of use or additional floors

E. Construction Support & Site Supervision

Hands-on quality control from foundation to finish.

  • Full-time and part-time site supervision and monitoring
  • Inspection of reinforcement, formwork and concrete placement
  • Review and approval of shop drawings and as-built drawings
  • On-site problem solving with contractors and sub-contractors
  • Concrete and material quality control guidance

F. Technical Documentation

Clear, complete deliverables for construction and regulatory approval.

  • Detailed structural drawings: foundation, framing, reinforcement
  • Bills of Quantities (BOQ) and material estimates
  • Technical reports, structural narratives and feasibility studies
  • Submission packages for regulatory approvals and enlistment

G. BIM & Digital Engineering

Digital-first delivery for better coordination and fewer site surprises.

  • 3D Building Information Modeling (BIM) using Autodesk Revit
  • Clash detection and coordination across disciplines
  • Digital delivery of project models for contractor use
  • Advanced 3D structural modeling for complex geometries

H. Soil Investigation & Geotechnical Services

PEC Service Code 0506 — from borehole to foundation recommendation.

  • Site soil investigation and subsurface exploration
  • Borehole drilling, soil sampling and Standard Penetration Testing (SPT)
  • Laboratory testing: grain size, Atterberg limits, compaction, shear strength
  • Geotechnical reports with bearing capacity and settlement analysis
  • Foundation recommendations: shallow, deep, raft and pile
  • Seismic site classification and liquefaction potential assessment
  • Groundwater depth assessment and dewatering recommendations
  • Slope stability analysis and earthwork support for retaining structures

I. Architectural Design Services

Integrated architectural design coordinated with structural and MEP disciplines.

  • Concept design and space planning for residential, commercial and institutional buildings
  • Floor plans, elevations, sections and 3D visualizations
  • Architectural-structural-MEP coordination for integrated delivery
  • Building permit and regulatory submission packages (CDA and local authorities)
  • Interior layout planning, facade design and material finish specifications

How We Work

Six-Step Design Methodology

Quality is embedded at every phase of the project lifecycle for consistent, safe and cost-effective outcomes.

01

Data Collection & Coordination

Reviewing architectural plans, understanding site constraints and soil conditions, engaging stakeholders and clarifying structural requirements.

02

Structural System Selection

Selecting the most efficient framing system considering seismic zone, occupancy type, functionality and cost optimization.

03

Advanced Structural Modeling

Detailed 3D models with comprehensive load combinations — gravity, wind, seismic, live loads — plus diaphragm and stiffness analysis.

04

Design Optimization

Balancing safety and economy through optimized reinforcement, section sizes and structural systems.

05

Detailed Drawings

Clear, contractor-friendly reinforcement and construction drawings, fully coordinated with architectural and MEP layouts.

06

Site Inspection & Quality Control

Regular site visits to verify compliance, monitor construction quality and resolve field issues with contractors.
