Who We Are

A One-Stop Structural Engineering Consultancy

Alpha Design Consultants (formerly Jadoon Consulting Engineers) is a Pakistan Engineering Council–registered consultancy headquartered in Rawalpindi/Islamabad with a regional office in Peshawar. We serve private developers, real estate firms, institutions, government agencies, NGOs and architects nationwide.

Reinforced concrete buildings of all occupancy types — residential, commercial, mixed-use and high-rise structures up to 20 floors.
