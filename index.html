About the Firm

Alpha Design Consultants (formerly Jadoon Consulting Engineers) is registered with the Pakistan Engineering Council. Our head office is located in Bahria Town, Rawalpindi, with a branch office in Hayatabad, Peshawar. We work with Developers, Architects, Institutions and Government Clients nationwide.

Structural Design

Reinforced concrete buildings of all occupancy types, residential, commercial, mixed-use and high-rise structures.

Earthquake Engineering

Seismic vulnerability assessment and earthquake-resistant design per ASCE 7, UBC and IBC, critical for Pakistan's high seismic-risk zones.

Design Vetting

Independent third-party review of structural safety, code compliance and cost optimization, with formal vetting reports for regulatory submission.

Retrofitting & Rehabilitation

Strengthening of earthquake-damaged or deteriorating structures: column jacketing, beam strengthening, shear walls and structural health reports.

Soil Investigation

Borehole drilling, SPT, laboratory testing and geotechnical reports with bearing capacity, settlement analysis and foundation recommendations.

Site Supervision

Full-time and part-time supervision, reinforcement, formwork and concrete inspection, shop drawing approvals and on-site problem solving.

BIM & Digital Engineering

3D Building Information Modeling in Autodesk Revit with clash detection, discipline coordination and digital handover.

Architectural Design

Concept design, floor plans, elevations, 3D visualizations and building permit submission packages for CDA and local authorities.

Interior Designing

Interior layouts, material and finish selection, ceiling and lighting schemes for residential and commercial spaces.

3D Modeling

Photorealistic 3D models and walkthroughs of buildings for design review, marketing and client approvals.

Quantity Surveying

Bills of Quantities (BOQ), Bar Bending Schedules (BBS) and Quantity Estimation (QE) for accurate costing and tendering.

QA/QC

Quality assurance and quality control plans, material testing coordination and site inspection checklists.

Surveying

Topographic and land surveys, site levelling and setting-out for construction works.

Building Construction

Grey structure and turnkey construction for houses and commercial buildings, executed under our own engineering supervision.
