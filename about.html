About Alpha Design Consultants

A PEC-registered structural and civil engineering consultancy with a reputation for technical accuracy, innovative design and client-focused service.

Company Overview

End-to-End Engineering, Under One Roof

Alpha Design Consultants (ADC) delivers end-to-end structural design, seismic engineering, soil investigation, third-party design vetting, site supervision and construction support services. Headquartered in Rawalpindi/Islamabad with a regional office in Peshawar, ADC has rapidly established a strong reputation across Pakistan.

Incorporated in 2022 under its former name — Jadoon Consulting Engineers (JCE) — the firm serves private developers, real estate firms, educational institutions, government agencies, NGOs and design architects nationwide.

Our portfolio spans high-rise commercial towers, hospitals, mosques, schools and government facilities across Islamabad, Peshawar, KP, Punjab, Rawalpindi, AJK and Sindh.

From JCE to Alpha Design Consultants

Effective 2026, Jadoon Consulting Engineers (JCE) was officially re-branded as Alpha Design Consultants (ADC), reflecting the firm's strategic growth and evolving identity as a comprehensive multi-disciplinary consultancy.

  • PEC Registration CONSULT/2371 — valid and unchanged
  • FBR registration active under the new entity name
  • All past projects, certifications and client relationships fully retained
  • Leadership, technical staff and methodology unchanged
