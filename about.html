About Alpha Design Consultants

PEC-registered structural and civil engineering consultancy based in Rawalpindi and Peshawar.

The firm

Alpha Design Consultants provides structural design, seismic engineering, soil investigation, design vetting, site supervision and construction support. The head office is in Bahria Town, Rawalpindi, with a branch office in Hayatabad, Peshawar.

Registered in 2022 as Jadoon Consulting Engineers (JCE), the firm now trades as Alpha Design Consultants. Clients include private developers, institutions, government bodies and architects.

Work to date includes high-rise towers, hospitals, mosques, schools and government buildings across Islamabad, Peshawar, KP, Punjab, Rawalpindi, AJK and Sindh.

From JCE to Alpha Design Consultants

Effective 2026, Jadoon Consulting Engineers (JCE) was officially re-branded as Alpha Design Consultants (ADC), reflecting the firm's strategic growth and evolving identity as a comprehensive multi-disciplinary consultancy.

  • PEC Registration CONSULT/2371, valid and unchanged
  • FBR registration active under the new entity name
  • All past projects, certifications and client relationships fully retained
  • Leadership, technical staff and methodology unchanged
